Borrow in four steps

Members can hold up to 3 books at a time.

  1. Search

    Look up a title, author or ISBN and check availability.

  2. Reserve

    Log in and reserve the book. It is held for you for 24 hours.

  3. Collect

    Show your student ID at the desk and take the book home.

  4. Return

    Bring it back within 14 days, or renew online once.
